# Activity A8 — Document the Continual Improvement Report

**Course:** ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System (C239)  
**Objective:** Document a QMS improvement activity report according to organisational procedures.

## Scenario

Three months after LPC launched its improvement programme, management needs evidence of results, unresolved risks and recommended next actions.

LionCity Precision Components Pte Ltd (LPC) is a singapore sme with 85 employees producing precision aluminium components for commercial equipment. Three regional equipment makers account for 72% of revenue; one now requires ISO 9001 certification from key suppliers.

## Task and expected output

Synthesize evidence from all prior activities into an accountable improvement report and management-review recommendation.

Produce: **A structured improvement report with baseline, analysis, decisions, responsibilities, evidence and lessons learned.**

## Decision questions

1. What changed relative to baseline and target, and how reliable is the evidence?
2. Which actions are complete, overdue, ineffective or awaiting evidence?
3. What should management decide about resources, changes and standardisation?

## Detailed procedure

1. Define the report audience, approval route and required organisational fields.
2. Summarise the original problem, baseline, scope and intended result.
3. Present analysis, actions, owners, dates and evidence of implementation.
4. Compare results with targets and state the effectiveness conclusion with limitations.
5. Record lessons, residual risks, decisions required and follow-up actions.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] A reviewer can reconstruct the improvement story from evidence and clearly see ownership, status, approval, effectiveness and next review date.
- [ ] Claims are traceable to the supplied evidence.
- [ ] Assumptions and missing evidence are labelled.
- [ ] The workbook contains an owner and next decision where applicable.
- [ ] A peer challenge and response are recorded.

## Facilitator debrief

Ask each team to defend one priority, control or conclusion using objective evidence. Probe what would change their decision. Connect the work to PDCA and the applicable QMS process rather than asking learners to recite clause text.
