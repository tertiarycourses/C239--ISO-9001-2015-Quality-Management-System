# Activity A1 — Diagnose Quality Gaps from Evidence

**Course:** ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System (C239)  
**Objective:** Identify areas for improvement by separating evidence, symptoms and assumptions.

## Scenario

LPC's customer complaints doubled in six months. Late deliveries, burr defects and inconsistent certificates are repeatedly mentioned, but managers disagree about the main cause.

LionCity Precision Components Pte Ltd (LPC) is a singapore sme with 85 employees producing precision aluminium components for commercial equipment. Three regional equipment makers account for 72% of revenue; one now requires ISO 9001 certification from key suppliers.

## Task and expected output

Analyse LPC's complaint, defect and delivery evidence; prioritise improvement areas and connect them to customer impact.

Produce: **A completed quality-gap prioritisation matrix and evidence-backed problem statement.**

## Decision questions

1. Which three issues should LPC prioritise, and what evidence supports each choice?
2. What is a symptom, what is an observed fact and what remains an assumption?
3. How does each issue affect conformity, customer satisfaction, cost or risk?

## Detailed procedure

1. Read the scenario and evidence table without proposing solutions yet.
2. Classify each item as fact, symptom, possible cause or missing evidence.
3. Score impact, frequency and evidence strength in the workbook.
4. Rank the top three gaps and write one measurable problem statement.
5. Peer-review the ranking and record one challenge to your reasoning.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] The top three gaps are traceable to supplied evidence; the problem statement contains a baseline, affected process and measurable effect.
- [ ] Claims are traceable to the supplied evidence.
- [ ] Assumptions and missing evidence are labelled.
- [ ] The workbook contains an owner and next decision where applicable.
- [ ] A peer challenge and response are recorded.

## Facilitator debrief

Ask each team to defend one priority, control or conclusion using objective evidence. Probe what would change their decision. Connect the work to PDCA and the applicable QMS process rather than asking learners to recite clause text.
