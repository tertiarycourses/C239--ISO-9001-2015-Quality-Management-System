# Activity A2 — Map and Improve the Order-to-Delivery Process

**Course:** ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System (C239)  
**Objective:** Perform process management and improvement using SIPOC, a process map and PDCA.

## Scenario

Sales accepts emailed drawings, Planning creates job travellers, Production machines parts, Quality inspects and Logistics ships. Rework and missing revision information frequently surface late.

LionCity Precision Components Pte Ltd (LPC) is a singapore sme with 85 employees producing precision aluminium components for commercial equipment. Three regional equipment makers account for 72% of revenue; one now requires ISO 9001 certification from key suppliers.

## Task and expected output

Map LPC's order-to-delivery process, expose hand-off risks and design a controlled future state.

Produce: **A SIPOC, current-state map, improvement hypothesis and PDCA action plan.**

## Decision questions

1. Where does value enter and leave the process, and who owns the end-to-end result?
2. Which hand-offs create the greatest risk of wrong revision, delay or rework?
3. What process measures will show whether the future state performs better?

## Detailed procedure

1. Define the process purpose, start/end points and customer outcome.
2. Complete the SIPOC before drawing detailed activities.
3. Map the current-state sequence, decisions, rework loops and evidence created.
4. Identify one bottleneck and one failure-demand loop using supplied data.
5. Design a future-state control and convert it into a PDCA experiment.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] The map has clear boundaries, owners, inputs, outputs, controls and measures; the PDCA experiment has a testable prediction.
- [ ] Claims are traceable to the supplied evidence.
- [ ] Assumptions and missing evidence are labelled.
- [ ] The workbook contains an owner and next decision where applicable.
- [ ] A peer challenge and response are recorded.

## Facilitator debrief

Ask each team to defend one priority, control or conclusion using objective evidence. Probe what would change their decision. Connect the work to PDCA and the applicable QMS process rather than asking learners to recite clause text.
