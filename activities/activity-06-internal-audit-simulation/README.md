# Activity A6 — Conduct an Internal Audit Simulation

**Course:** ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System (C239)  
**Objective:** Assess QMS compliance through objective evidence, sampling and impartial reporting.

## Scenario

A customer returned parts made to an obsolete drawing revision. LPC needs an impartial internal audit before management review.

LionCity Precision Components Pte Ltd (LPC) is a singapore sme with 85 employees producing precision aluminium components for commercial equipment. Three regional equipment makers account for 72% of revenue; one now requires ISO 9001 certification from key suppliers.

## Task and expected output

Plan and conduct a simulated internal audit of LPC's order-review and production-control process.

Produce: **An audit plan, process-based checklist, evidence log and correctly worded findings.**

## Decision questions

1. What audit trail follows the customer requirement through planning, production, inspection and release?
2. What makes evidence objective and the sample appropriate?
3. Does each finding state requirement, evidence and the gap without prescribing a solution?

## Detailed procedure

1. Set audit objectives, scope, criteria, timing and auditor independence.
2. Prepare open questions that follow the process and sample records.
3. Interview role-players and record exact evidence, not conclusions alone.
4. Triangulate documents, interviews and observations.
5. Classify and write findings; conduct a concise closing meeting.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] The audit trail is reproducible and every nonconformity statement includes the requirement, objective evidence and unmet condition.
- [ ] Claims are traceable to the supplied evidence.
- [ ] Assumptions and missing evidence are labelled.
- [ ] The workbook contains an owner and next decision where applicable.
- [ ] A peer challenge and response are recorded.

## Facilitator debrief

Ask each team to defend one priority, control or conclusion using objective evidence. Probe what would change their decision. Connect the work to PDCA and the applicable QMS process rather than asking learners to recite clause text.
