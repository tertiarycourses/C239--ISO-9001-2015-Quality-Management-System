# Activity A7 — Follow Up a Nonconformity and Corrective Action

**Course:** ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System (C239)  
**Objective:** Follow up improvement from containment through root cause, action and effectiveness review.

## Scenario

Twenty-four parts were produced to revision B after the customer approved revision C. Eight were shipped before the error was detected.

LionCity Precision Components Pte Ltd (LPC) is a singapore sme with 85 employees producing precision aluminium components for commercial equipment. Three regional equipment makers account for 72% of revenue; one now requires ISO 9001 certification from key suppliers.

## Task and expected output

Manage LPC's obsolete-drawing nonconformity using containment, 5 Whys, action planning and effectiveness criteria.

Produce: **A complete NCR/CAPA record with evidence-based root cause and an effectiveness review plan.**

## Decision questions

1. What correction and containment are needed immediately?
2. What system cause allowed the obsolete revision to reach production?
3. What evidence and time window will demonstrate non-recurrence?

## Detailed procedure

1. Record the nonconformity, affected scope, risk and immediate correction.
2. Contain suspect product and protect the customer while tracing affected records.
3. Use 5 Whys and cause-and-effect thinking; validate the systemic root cause with evidence.
4. Assign corrective actions to causes with owners, dates and required resources.
5. Define and later perform an effectiveness review using trend and audit evidence.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Actions address verified causes, not only symptoms; the effectiveness criterion is measurable and scheduled after sufficient evidence can accumulate.
- [ ] Claims are traceable to the supplied evidence.
- [ ] Assumptions and missing evidence are labelled.
- [ ] The workbook contains an owner and next decision where applicable.
- [ ] A peer challenge and response are recorded.
