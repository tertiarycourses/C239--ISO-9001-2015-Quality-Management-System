# Activity A5 — Evaluate a Process Against QMS Requirements

**Course:** ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System (C239)  
**Objective:** Evaluate a process by linking requirements, controls, evidence and results.

## Scenario

Supplier approvals exist, but several files are incomplete and incoming defects have increased. Procurement says the process is compliant because a procedure exists.

LionCity Precision Components Pte Ltd (LPC) is a singapore sme with 85 employees producing precision aluminium components for commercial equipment. Three regional equipment makers account for 72% of revenue; one now requires ISO 9001 certification from key suppliers.

## Task and expected output

Assess LPC's supplier-control process using a requirements-to-evidence matrix without treating a checklist as proof.

Produce: **A clause applicability and evidence matrix with conformity judgements and follow-up questions.**

## Decision questions

1. What evidence shows implementation and effectiveness rather than documentation alone?
2. Which requirements are applicable to this process and why?
3. What additional evidence is needed before reaching a conclusion?

## Detailed procedure

1. Define the process purpose, intended results and applicable requirements.
2. List the control claimed by LPC and the objective evidence available.
3. Rate evidence for relevance, reliability and sufficiency.
4. Compare actual results with planned results and requirements.
5. Record conformity, concern or evidence gap without inventing facts.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Each judgement cites a requirement, evidence and result; unknowns remain evidence gaps rather than automatic nonconformities.
- [ ] Claims are traceable to the supplied evidence.
- [ ] Assumptions and missing evidence are labelled.
- [ ] The workbook contains an owner and next decision where applicable.
- [ ] A peer challenge and response are recorded.

## Facilitator debrief

Ask each team to defend one priority, control or conclusion using objective evidence. Probe what would change their decision. Connect the work to PDCA and the applicable QMS process rather than asking learners to recite clause text.
